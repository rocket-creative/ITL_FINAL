## The Math

You have **204 pages** on your sitemap. Posting 3 days per week across 5 platforms gives you **15 post slots per week**. Each post links to a different page.

- 204 pages / 15 links per week = **~14 weeks to hit every page once**
- That means every page on your site gets a social backlink within one quarter
- Then you cycle back and hit them all again with fresh copy

This is how you build authority. Google sees consistent, diverse backlinks pointing to deep pages across your site — not just your homepage.

## Instagram Note

Instagram requires a visual to post. Since you're not creating graphics, here are zero cost options:

- **Text on a plain background** — Use Instagram's built in text post feature or the free Canva app to type your post text onto a solid branded background color. Takes 30 seconds.
- **Lab photos from your phone** — Snap a quick photo of the lab, equipment, or team. Pair it with your text caption. Authentic content performs well on Instagram.
- **Screenshot of your website page** — Screenshot the page you're linking to. Simple, fast, and it shows people what they're clicking through to.

None of these require a designer or budget.

## Writing Posts at Scale

To make 15 posts per week sustainable, use this system:

**Batch writing sessions:** Sit down once per week (or every two weeks) and write all your posts in advance. Use the weekly theme from the rotation above so you're thinking about one topic cluster at a time.

**The formula for every post:**

1. **Hook** — A surprising fact, bold statement, or question that makes someone stop scrolling
2. **Context** — 1 to 3 sentences explaining why this matters to a researcher
3. **Link** — The URL to the page on your site

That's it. No calls to action like "contact us today!" — just make the content interesting enough that people want to click.

**Adapt length by platform:**
- LinkedIn: 150 to 250 words (long form works)
- Facebook: 50 to 100 words
- Twitter: Under 280 characters
- Threads: 1 to 3 sentences
- Instagram: 2 to 4 sentence caption + "Link in bio"

**One post, five versions.** Write the LinkedIn version first (longest), then trim it down for Facebook, then trim again for Twitter/Threads, then adapt for Instagram caption. One writing session produces all five.

## Tracking What Works

**Weekly (5 minutes):**
- Check which posts got the most engagement on each platform
- Note which page links got the most clicks (use UTM parameters on your URLs to track this in Google Analytics)

**Monthly (30 minutes):**
- Referral traffic by platform in Google Analytics
- Which site pages are getting the most social traffic
- Adjust the next cycle's post copy based on what topics resonated

**UTM format for tracking:**
Use this structure on every link so you can track everything in GA:

`genetargeting.com/conditional-knockout-mouse-models?utm_source=linkedin&utm_medium=social&utm_campaign=week1`
